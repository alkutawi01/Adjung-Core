# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Frontpage of Adjung.

The Frontpage is the primary public gateway to the platform and should
introduce knowledge, authors, and publications without adopting the
behaviour of a social media feed.

# 2. Philosophy

The Frontpage should encourage discovery through relevance, quality, and
diversity rather than addictive engagement.

It is the entrance to an intellectual ecosystem.

# 3. Objectives

The Frontpage shall:

- Introduce new visitors to Adjung.
- Surface high-quality knowledge.
- Promote meaningful discovery.
- Highlight authors and curated collections.
- Reflect the identity of the platform.

# 4. Primary Sections

The Frontpage may include:

- Featured Publications
- Featured Folios
- Discover Topics
- Editorial Selections
- Recent Publications
- Recommended Reading
- Search Entry
- Platform Announcements

# 5. Content Selection Principles

Displayed content should prioritise:

- Editorial quality
- Relevance
- Diversity of subjects
- Freshness where appropriate
- Long-term value

Popularity alone must never determine visibility.

# 6. User Experience

The Frontpage should be:

- Calm
- Accessible
- Predictable
- Fast
- Distraction-free

# 7. Accessibility

Core information must remain accessible without unnecessary scrolling,
animations, or visual clutter.

# 8. Future Expansion

Future capabilities may include:

- Personalised discovery
- Institutional highlights
- Knowledge collections
- Seasonal editorials
- Community spotlights
