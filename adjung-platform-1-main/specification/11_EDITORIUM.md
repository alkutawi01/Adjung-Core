# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Editorium, the editorial environment of
Adjung.

Editorium governs editorial activities while preserving author ownership
and intellectual integrity.

# 2. Philosophy

Publishing and editing are complementary but distinct activities.

Editors improve clarity, consistency, and quality without altering an
author’s intended meaning.

# 3. Objectives

The Editorium shall:

- Support editorial review.
- Encourage high editorial standards.
- Preserve transparent revision history.
- Promote constructive collaboration.

# 4. Editorial Roles

Typical roles may include:

- Author
- Reviewer
- Editor
- Managing Editor
- Administrator

Role definitions are specified separately in the RBAC specification.

# 5. Editorial Workflow

Typical workflow:

Submission → Editorial Review → Author Revision → Approval → Publication

# 6. Editorial Principles

Editors should prioritise:

- Accuracy
- Clarity
- Consistency
- Attribution
- Respect for author intent

# 7. Editorial Records

Editorial actions should be auditable where appropriate, including:

- Reviews
- Decisions
- Revision requests
- Publication approvals

# 8. Relationship with Other Modules

Editorium integrates with:

- Publication Model
- Writing Desk
- Identity System
- Access Policy

# 9. Future Expansion

Potential future capabilities include:

- Peer review
- Blind review
- Editorial boards
- Institutional workflows
- Quality assurance dashboards
