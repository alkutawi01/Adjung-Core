# PLATFORM CONTENT POLICY

Document ID: SPEC-023
Version: 1.0 (Draft)
Status: Official Draft

Depends On:
- SPEC-000 Adjung Constitution
- SPEC-001 Product Philosophy

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official content policy of the Adjung platform. It specifies what content is permitted, what is prohibited, and what content may require review.

# 2. Philosophy

Adjung is a knowledge publishing platform.

The platform does not act as a judge over all scholarly disagreements.

Instead, it protects the integrity of knowledge, user safety, and legal compliance.

# 3. Core Principles

The platform upholds the following principles:

- Intellectual freedom
- Respectful discourse
- Evidence-based publishing
- Author responsibility
- Transparent moderation
- Minimal intervention

# 4. Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited and will not be tolerated on the platform:

- Spam
- Scam and fraudulent schemes
- Phishing attempts
- Malware distribution
- Pornography
- Sexual exploitation
- CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material)
- Doxxing
- Threats of violence or harm
- Incitement to violence
- Illegal content
- Identity impersonation
- Intentional plagiarism
- Platform manipulation
- Misuse of AI to deceive readers

# 5. Reviewable Content

This category encompasses content that is not necessarily deleted outright but is subject to review upon being reported.

Reviewable content includes:

- Factual claims made without citations or sources
- Disputed quotations
- Fabricated or falsely attributed hadith
- Harmful or dangerous health information
- Content that may potentially mislead the public
- Potential defamation

Reviews are conducted based on evidence and adherence to platform policies.

The platform does not make binding decisions on pure academic disagreements.

# 6. Scholarly Disagreement

The following scenarios do not constitute policy violations:

- Differences in schools of thought (mazhab)
- Differences in interpretation
- Scholarly criticism
- Academic debate
- Hypotheses
- Novel or unorthodox theories
- Counter-arguments

Users are strongly encouraged to respond to scholarly disagreements by writing a Note or an Essay, rather than requesting the platform to remove the content.

# 7. Author Responsibility

Authors bear full responsibility for the accuracy of the claims they publish.

The platform does not guarantee that all published content is inherently true or verified.

# 8. Enforcement

For the detailed execution of moderation actions and workflows, refer to SPEC-024 (Moderation & Enforcement Policy).

# 9. Future Expansion

Future expansions of the content policy may adapt to new forms of media, evolving legal requirements, and advanced community self-governance frameworks, though the core principles of intellectual freedom and minimal intervention will remain unchanged.
