# WRITING DESK

Document ID: SPEC-008 Version: 1.0 (Draft) Status: Official Draft

Depends On: - SPEC-000 Adjung Constitution - SPEC-001 Product
Philosophy - SPEC-007 Publication Model

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Writing Desk, the primary authoring
environment of Adjung.

The Writing Desk is designed for thoughtful knowledge creation rather
than fast content posting.

# 2. Philosophy

Writing should be calm, structured, and distraction-free.

The editor must help authors think clearly before publishing.

# 3. Primary Objectives

- Encourage deep writing
- Reduce unnecessary distractions
- Preserve author intent
- Support long-form and short-form publications
- Maintain editorial quality

# 4. Supported Publication Types

- Note
- Essay

Additional publication types shall be introduced only through formal
specification.

# 5. Core Capabilities

The Writing Desk should support:

- Rich text editing
- Autosave
- Draft management
- Version history
- Footnotes
- Citations
- References
- Images
- Tables
- Lists
- Quotes

# 6. Draft Management

Every publication begins as a draft.

Drafts are private until intentionally published.

# 7. Publishing Workflow

Typical workflow:

Draft → Review → Preview → Publish → Revise → Archive

# 8. AI Assistance

Artificial Intelligence assists authors by improving clarity, grammar,
structure, and consistency.

AI must never publish on behalf of an author without explicit
confirmation.

# 9. Editorial Principles

The editor should prioritise:

- Clarity
- Accessibility
- Consistency
- Readability
- Preservation

# 10. Future Expansion

Future capabilities may include:

- Collaborative editing
- Offline editing
- Templates
- Research mode
- Focus mode
- Integrated bibliography
- Annotation tools
