# ADJUNG CONSTITUTION

Document ID: SPEC-000  
Version: 1.1 (Draft)  
Status: Official Draft

## Preamble

Adjung is an open knowledge publishing ecosystem dedicated to preserving,
developing, and disseminating human knowledge in a structured,
accessible, and enduring manner.

Adjung is founded on the belief that every individual deserves an
intellectual space that respects authorship, critical thinking, and the
long-term value of written work, regardless of academic background,
profession, or social status.

Adjung is not designed to be a social network, a blogging platform, or
merely an academic journal repository. Instead, it is a long-term
knowledge publishing ecosystem centred on intellectual quality, editorial
integrity, and long-term preservation.

This Constitution is the supreme governing document of the Adjung
platform. Every specification, architectural decision, implementation,
interface, AI behaviour, and governance policy shall conform to this
Constitution.

## 7. Platform Architecture and Topology

### 7.1. The Author Site

Every registered Author within the Adjung ecosystem is assigned exactly ONE Author Site. The Author Site serves as the author's permanent intellectual presence on the platform.

- **Canonical Identity**: The canonical URL for an Author Site is defined strictly by the subdomain structure: `username.Adjung.com`. External custom domains are intentionally not supported to ensure long-term stability and ecological cohesion.
- **Platform Governance**: The Author Site is hosted and governed by the Adjung platform. While Authors retain full ownership of their intellectual content, Adjung governs the platform architecture, typography standards, navigation models, routing policies, long-term preservation, and the overall user experience.
- **Core Site Anatomy**: Every Author Site consists of exactly four primary pages. No additional primary pages or sections may be introduced without a revision to the official platform specifications:
  1. **Folio**: The homepage of the Author Site, displaying a curated selection of the author's work.
  2. **Biography**: A permanent, dedicated page describing the author's scholarly background and context.
  3. **Desk**: The private, secure workspace where the author creates, drafts, and manages their works.
  4. **Account**: The administrative page containing account settings and platform-permitted site parameters.

### 7.2. Publication Objects

Adjung officially recognises only three distinct publication types:
- **Note**: A concise publication intended for brief reflections, observations, annotations, or structured ideas.
- **Essay**: A long-form structured publication intended for deep exposition, storytelling, argument, or comprehensive analysis.
- **Article**: A formal publication intended for academic, technical, professional, or research-oriented writing.

These publication types are strictly defined as publication objects, not distinct pages. They are managed through the author's private Desk and displayed dynamically through their public Folio.

### 7.3. The Ecosystem Portal

The address `Adjung.com` serves as the public ecosystem portal. It is not an individual author's primary site; rather, it functions as the central gateway that connects all individual Author Sites, facilitating discovery, cross-referencing, indexing, and scholarly synthesis across the entire Adjung publishing ecosystem.
