# BIOGRAPHY

Document ID: SPEC-010 Version: 1.0 (Draft) Status: Official Draft

Depends On: - SPEC-000 Adjung Constitution - SPEC-001 Product
Philosophy - SPEC-006 Identity System - SPEC-009 Folio

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Biography component of Adjung.

A Biography provides the personal and professional context behind an
author’s intellectual work.

# 2. Philosophy

Biography explains the person.

Folio demonstrates the person’s intellectual contributions.

These two concepts complement each other but must remain distinct.

# 3. Objectives

The Biography system shall:

- Present an author’s background.
- Establish credibility through transparent context.
- Help readers understand the author’s perspective.
- Support long-term professional identity.

# 4. Core Sections

A Biography may include:

- Display name
- Professional title
- Short biography
- Extended biography
- Education
- Professional experience
- Research interests
- Areas of expertise
- Awards
- External links

# 5. Privacy

Authors control which biography fields are public.

Sensitive or private information must never be exposed without explicit
consent.

# 6. Relationship with Other Modules

Biography integrates with:

- Identity System
- Folio
- Publications
- Editorium

Changes to a Biography do not alter published works.

# 7. Design Principles

A Biography should be:

- Authentic
- Accurate
- Professional
- Readable
- Timeless

# 8. Future Expansion

Future capabilities may include:

- Timeline view
- Career milestones
- Verified affiliations
- ORCID integration
- Institutional profiles
- Multimedia biography
