# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Folio system of Adjung.

A Folio is a curated intellectual portfolio that enables authors to
organise and present their work around themes, expertise, projects, or
purposes.

# 2. Philosophy

A Folio is not merely a list of publications.

It represents an author’s intentional narrative and intellectual
identity.

# 3. Objectives

The Folio system shall:

- Showcase an author’s best work.
- Organise publications into meaningful collections.
- Support long-term professional and intellectual development.
- Improve discoverability without relying on popularity metrics.

# 4. Core Elements

A Folio may contain:

- Publications
- Collections
- Featured works
- Introduction
- Cover image
- Tags
- External references

# 5. Ownership

Every Folio belongs to its creator.

Authors retain full control over the structure and presentation of their
Folios, subject to platform policies.

# 6. Visibility

Supported visibility levels include:

- Private
- Unlisted
- Public

Future access policies may extend these options.

# 7. Design Principles

A Folio should be:

- Curated
- Readable
- Consistent
- Timeless
- Accessible

# 8. Relationships

A publication may appear in multiple Folios without duplication.

Removing a publication from a Folio must not affect the original
publication.

# 9. Future Expansion

Future capabilities may include:

- Institutional Folios
- Team Folios
- Showcase templates
- Portfolio analytics
- Export and archival packages
