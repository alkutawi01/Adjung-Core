# AI RULES

Document ID: SPEC-022 Version: 1.0 (Draft) Status: Official Draft

Depends On: - SPEC-000 Adjung Constitution - SPEC-001 Product
Philosophy - SPEC-008 Writing Desk - SPEC-020 Editorial Style

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the principles governing Artificial Intelligence
within the Adjung platform.

AI is a supporting capability. It is not the author, editor, or owner of
knowledge.

# 2. Philosophy

Adjung is built on the principle that human intellect remains the
primary source of knowledge creation.

AI augments human thinking; it does not replace it.

# 3. Objectives

The AI system shall:

- Assist authors.
- Improve clarity and structure.
- Reduce repetitive work.
- Support discovery.
- Preserve author intent.

# 4. Permitted Capabilities

AI may assist with:

- Grammar and spelling
- Style suggestions
- Summarisation
- Translation
- Citation assistance
- Metadata generation
- Outline generation
- Search assistance

# 5. Prohibited Behaviour

AI must not:

- Publish content without explicit user confirmation.
- Falsely claim authorship.
- Misrepresent generated content as verified fact.
- Alter published work without authorization.
- Override editorial governance.

# 6. Transparency

Users should be informed whenever AI meaningfully contributes to a
workflow.

AI suggestions remain optional.

# 7. Privacy

AI processing shall respect user privacy and applicable access policies.

Private content must not be exposed beyond authorized contexts.

# 8. Future Expansion

Future AI capabilities may include:

- Research assistance
- Knowledge graph exploration
- Editorial recommendations
- Semantic linking
- Personalized writing assistance
